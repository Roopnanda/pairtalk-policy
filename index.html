Privacy Policy for PairTalk

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

PairTalk ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our services.

1. Information Collection & Usage

PairTalk does not require personal account creation, names, phone numbers, or social media logins to access core voice-matching services.

  • Voice & Audio Data: Audio calls are transmitted in real-time using peer-to-peer WebRTC technology. We do not record, store, or monitor your voice conversations on our servers.
  • Device & Technical Data: We may collect non-personally identifiable technical details, such as unique hardware identifiers, operating system version, and general network state. This information is processed strictly to maintain matchmaking stability, manage queues, and prevent spam or abusive conduct.

2. Device Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required exclusively to capture real-time audio during active call sessions. Audio data is never saved to local device storage or remote databases.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Utilized solely to provide foreground service status during calls and notify users of active connection events.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

PairTalk utilizes third-party advertising partners, including Google AdMob, to serve advertisements. These service providers may collect and process anonymous advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) and device performance metrics in accordance with their respective privacy policies to deliver relevant ads and detect fraud.

4. User Safety, Moderation & Reporting

To maintain a safe and constructive learning environment, PairTalk features an in-app reporting system. Reports submitted regarding harassment, inappropriate behavior, or community violations are reviewed based on technical session tokens. Repeated or severe infractions will result in temporary or permanent device lockout.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect personal identities, user profiles, or store call contents, we do not maintain personal user databases. Anonymous technical identifiers used for matchmaking and moderation logs are periodically purged. For data handling inquiries, reach out via our contact email below.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be posted directly to this public URL with an updated effective date.
